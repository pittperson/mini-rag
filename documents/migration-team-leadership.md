## Situation
A major banking customer required large-scale storage reclamation and migration across its enterprise data center environment. The work involved a regulated and highly audited financial-services environment where storage changes had to be carefully planned, documented, validated, and executed because configuration errors could create operational outages, data-access issues, or compliance concerns.

The engagement involved complex enterprise storage environments supporting multiple storage protocols and architectures, including Fibre Channel (FC), iSCSI, and NAS. The customer needed to migrate and reclaim existing storage resources while deploying new storage infrastructure, creating a significant amount of discovery, planning, documentation, and coordination work.

The scale and complexity of the environment made a heavily manual migration process inefficient and susceptible to human error. Accurate storage configuration information had to be collected and transformed into usable migration documentation before engineers could safely execute the required changes.

## Action
Served as the technical lead for a five-engineer NetApp Professional Services team responsible for deploying new storage infrastructure and executing storage migration and reclamation activities across the customer's enterprise data centers.

Provided technical direction to the engineering team while coordinating the storage deployment and migration activities required to transition workloads and reclaim existing storage resources. Worked across multiple storage architectures, including Fibre Channel (FC), iSCSI, and NAS, requiring an understanding of enterprise storage connectivity, configuration, dependencies, and migration planning.

Identified repetitive manual work in the storage discovery and migration-planning process and developed an automation solution using Perl, Bash, and NetApp SDKs. Built an ETL-style workflow that extracted storage configuration information from the environment, transformed and organized that information, and generated structured migration documentation for use by the engineering team.

The automation converted raw storage configuration data into actionable migration documentation, reducing the amount of manual data collection and document preparation required from engineers. This allowed the team to spend more time executing and validating migration activities rather than manually gathering and formatting storage information.

Combined hands-on technical engineering with team leadership, process improvement, scripting, automation, migration planning, and customer-facing Professional Services responsibilities.

## Result
Successfully delivered complex storage migration and reclamation activities across the bank's enterprise data center environment while leading a five-person engineering team.

The engagement supported multiple storage protocols and architectures, including FC, iSCSI, and NAS, while maintaining the planning, documentation, and execution discipline required in a regulated financial-services environment.

The automation replaced a repetitive, manual, and error-prone documentation process with a repeatable workflow based on Perl, Bash, NetApp SDKs, and ETL techniques. By automating the transformation of storage configuration data into migration documentation, the team reduced manual effort and lowered the operational risk associated with manually collecting and transcribing large amounts of infrastructure data.

The engagement demonstrated the ability to combine technical leadership, enterprise storage expertise, automation, and process improvement to execute complex infrastructure projects in environments where accuracy, repeatability, and documentation are critical.
